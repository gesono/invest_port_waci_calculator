# Where to find a fund's WACI

Fund managers publish carbon intensity in different places and formats. These notes cover the sources checked so far. Always enter the figure **exactly as published**, with its scope, currency (per $1m or €1m of revenue), coverage and date.

## Monthly reports and factsheets

| Fund manager | Where to look | What it's called | Typical format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OP (Finland)** | Fund page on op.fi → *Monthly review*. The ESG section is in the **Finnish** monthly report (*Kuukausikatsaus*) | *ESG-analyysi* box → **Hiili-intensiteetti**; coverage shown as *Kattavuus* | Scope 1+2, tCO2e per $1m revenue |
| **iShares (BlackRock)** | Fund factsheet **for a non-UK country** (for example the Finnish *en-fi* version), or the fund's product web page. The UK (*en-gb*) factsheet may omit carbon data | *Sustainability Characteristics* → **MSCI Weighted Average Carbon Intensity (Tons CO2E/$M SALES)** and its *% Coverage* | Scope 1+2, tCO2e per $1m sales |
| **Other managers** | Monthly factsheet, often in an "ESG" or "Sustainability" section | Carbon intensity, WACI, weighted average carbon intensity | Check scope and currency each time |

iShares factsheets also state the data dates, typically as "MSCI ESG Fund Ratings as of …, based on holdings as of …". Use the holdings date where both are given.

## SFDR periodic reports (Scope 1+2+3)

Funds classified under SFDR **Article 8 or 9** publish an annual periodic report. If the fund considers principal adverse impacts, it includes:

| Indicator | Use in the calculator |
|---|---|
| **PAI 3 – GHG intensity of investee companies** | Enter in the **Scope 1+2+3** column, marked **per €1m revenue** |
| PAI 2 – Carbon footprint | Not WACI (it is per €1m *invested*); do not enter |
| PAI 1 – GHG emissions | Absolute tonnes; do not enter |

These reports are published once a year, so their figures can be up to 12–18 months old.

## Checklist before entering a figure

- [ ] Is it **WACI** (per $1m or €1m of **revenue/sales**), not carbon footprint (per $1m or €1m **invested**)?
- [ ] Which **scope**: 1+2 or 1+2+3?
- [ ] Per **$1m** or **€1m**?
- [ ] What **date** does it refer to?
- [ ] Is a **coverage** percentage given?
