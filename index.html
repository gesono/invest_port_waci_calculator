Work out the carbon intensity of your fund portfolio

Enter each fund you hold, how much you have in it, and the carbon intensity (WACI) from its factsheet or monthly report. The calculator does the rest. Nothing you type leaves this browser.

Your funds

Your portfolio's carbon intensity

How this is calculated

  • Portfolio WACI = sum of (each fund's share × its WACI). Shares are rescaled over the funds that have a figure, so they add up to 100%. Portfolio coverage shows how much of your money that is.
  • Scope 1+2 and Scope 1+2+3 are calculated separately and never combined.
  • Figures are used as published. If some funds report per $1m and others per €1m of revenue, the result mixes the two and is flagged, because the same company looks roughly 15% different depending on the currency.
  • Data coverage is the share of each fund's own holdings that its WACI is based on, as the fund reports it. The weighted figure averages it across your funds.
  • Below 70% portfolio coverage, a result is marked indicative. Funds without a figure are listed, never estimated.
  • This aggregates figures that funds already publish. It is not a company-by-company PCAF calculation.
  • For information only, not investment advice.
